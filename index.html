Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about admissions, programs, and campus life at ACT.

What programs does ACT provide?

ACT offers accredited undergraduate programs in BSc in Computer Science and BA in Business Administration, as well as graduate programs including MBA (Regular, Online, and International), MSc in Computer Science, and Online Master's in Project Management. We also provide over 100 professional training courses in technology, business, leadership, and soft skills.

Is ACT accredited?

Yes, ACT is a fully accredited higher education institution recognized by the Education and Training Authority (ETA). Our programs meet both national and international quality standards.

How can I apply for admission?

You can apply online through our website by visiting the Apply Now page. Create an account, fill out the application form, upload the required documents (transcripts, matric results, national ID, and passport photos), and submit. Initial review typically takes 5–7 business days.

Can I transfer credits from another institution?

Yes, ACT accepts transfer credits from accredited institutions on a case-by-case basis. You will need to provide official transcripts and course descriptions from your previous institution for evaluation by our academic committee.

What makes ACT different from other institutions?

ACT stands out for its strong industry partnerships with global organizations such as Oracle, Cisco, Coursera, Huawei, ICDL, and Infosec, providing students with internationally recognized certifications alongside their degrees.

Can training courses be customized for organizations?

Absolutely. ACT specializes in designing tailored training programs for corporate clients, government agencies, and NGOs, including organizations such as Ethiopian Airlines, Ethiotelecom, and Dashen Bank.
